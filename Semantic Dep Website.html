About Department

Vision

To emerge as a premier center of computer applications education and research, developing ethical, innovative, and globally competent technology leaders.

Mission

To provide high-quality education through modern curricula and state-of-the-art laboratories.
To foster research, problem-solving abilities, and industry collaborations among students.
To instill professional ethics, team spirit, and lifelong learning for societal advancement.

Objectives

1. Equip students with core software development skills and emerging industry frameworks.
2. Bridge academia and industry through guest lectures, hackathons, and industrial internships.
3. Encourage interdisciplinary research projects and higher education pursuits.
